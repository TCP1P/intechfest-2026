Community beatmaps
------------------

Rythme selects exact, unmodified community beatmap difficulties. Each reveal
shows the community metadata, mapper, ruleset, difficulty, star rating,
BeatmapSetID, BeatmapID, official page, archive source, and pinned SHA-256.
Rythme does not replace the hit objects with generated charts.

Beatmap and music rights remain with their respective creators and rights
holders. Inclusion in a public beatmap listing does not itself grant general
redistribution rights. The source repository therefore does not bundle these
.osz archives. Event operators must review the selected maps, obtain any
permission required for their event, and populate the optional coordinator
cache only when permitted.

The event client normally retrieves each exact archive directly from the
pinned HTTPS source, using a separate connection without event credentials or
cookies. It rejects redirects and accepts an archive only after SHA-256
verification. The coordinator provides a verified fallback from its organizer
cache. The current source is the third-party Catboy beatmap mirror, which can
observe the player's source IP. Catboy is not operated by ppy or by Rythme, and
its availability does not change the pinned BeatmapSetID, BeatmapID, or
SHA-256 validation.

Organizer song list
-------------------

The organizer supplied titles, artists, albums, dates, and durations as
curation metadata. Rythme uses no Spotify audio, artwork, API response, access
token, application credential, or player session.

Some community maps use an original recording or another cover of the supplied
song. Rythme preserves the map's actual metadata and records that distinction
in the author source ledger. It does not claim that two recordings are the
same asset.
